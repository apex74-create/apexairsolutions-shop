The offer — what this property carries

  • The name and the store. The domain apexairsolutions.shop transfers to the buyer, with this storefront — plain static files in a repository the buyer owns. What you are reading is the asset, standing.
  • The stocked shelves. The full application package above: the live products, the depot's fifteen faces, and the in-build lines at their stated states.
  • Twelve new applications in year one — one a month, guaranteed as part of this property's terms, landing on the twelve shelves above.
  • Your own build, every month — for twelve months you scope one application and the house builds it. Your store bends to your market.
  • Beyond the package: extra applications are priced per application, and custom scope work is called in at the house's published rates (websites from $375 · Starter storefront site $549 · determinations $125 — the Apex Ground Crew sheet).
  • Year two onward: the monthly supply continues as a subscription service, so the store never stops being fed.
  • Your side of the fence: you run your own hosting and you drive your own traffic. Building the store and stocking it is the house's work; selling from it is yours.

This property is offered for sale as one complete unit through the house's auction listing. The full plain-words terms — including what stays licensed — are on the What Is Real Here page, and they are part of this asset.

The engine — stated in the open

The applications on these shelves run on Apex API calls — the house's engines do the crunch behind them. That arrangement is this property's meter, and it is disclosed here, on the storefront itself, before any money moves:

  • The site is yours. The engine is licensed, never sold. Calls are metered under a license key — keyed to you, expiring, and revocable.
  • The rates are published — the house's standing rate card for application calls:
TierRateBuys
Participant$12 / domain / month, billed yearly ($15 month-to-month)Authorized calls at the base depth
Intel$29 / month (up to 3 domains)Greater call depth and freshness

Enterprise$749 / year + $3 / site / month wholesaleFleet-scale licensing
  • If a license lapses, your pages and your store remain entirely yours — the engine calls stop until the license is renewed. Nothing you own is held hostage; the meter simply stops turning.

Nothing about this is hidden in fine print: the dependency is the deal, printed on the front of the store. A buyer who wants a property with no engine license at all should ask the house about a clean-title arrangement before bidding — it is a different property at a different price.

What it costs to run

  • Hosting — yours. This storefront is static files; it runs on any static host, including free tiers. Whatever you choose to spend beyond that is your call.
  • The name — yours. The domain renews at the registrar's standard rate, on your account, after transfer.
  • The engine license — metered. The rate card above, at the tier your traffic needs. That is the whole of the house's ongoing take.
  • Traffic — yours. The house builds and stocks the store. Customers are the buyer's department, and no page on this site pretends otherwise.
