A · What is real on this property

Every product named on the storefront is a real Apex Air Solutions build, and each shelf tile states that build's true standing as of Nov 12, 2026: two products live, two extensions submitted to the Chrome Web Store and pending Google's review that day, one Android build packaged and in field testing, two product lines in build with their pages live, and fifteen watch faces in the Application Depot catalogue — three landed, twelve built — at $9.99 each.

No sales figures, visitor counts, testimonials, or revenue numbers appear anywhere on this property, because there are none to state: this storefront is newly built and has made no sales. Any future figure a seller quotes you that is not on this page is not the house's figure.

B · What you get

  • The domain name apexairsolutions.shop, transferred to your registrar account.
  • This storefront — the exact pages you are reading — as plain static files in a code repository that becomes yours. No framework, no build step, nothing between you and the files.
  • The stocked shelves — the application package as listed, at the states stated.
  • The first-year supply — twelve new applications, one a month, plus one application a month that you scope and the house builds, for twelve months.
  • An engine license at the published rate card, at the tier you choose.

C · What it costs to run

  • Hosting: yours. The site is static files and runs on any static host, free tiers included.
  • Domain renewal: yours, at your registrar's standard rate.
  • Engine license: the published rate card (Participant $12/domain/month billed yearly · Intel $29/month · Intel+ $79/month · Enterprise $749/year + $3/site/month). This is the house's entire ongoing take from the property.
  • Everything past the package — extra applications per application, custom scope work at the house's published rates — is bought only when you call it in.

D · What stays licensed — in the open

  • The applications call the house's engines (Apex API calls). The site is yours; the engine is licensed, never sold. The license key is keyed to the licensee, expires, and is revocable — the same calibration the house states for its own security products.
  • If the license lapses or is revoked for non-payment, your store, your pages, and your name remain entirely yours. The engine calls stop until the license is made good. That is the whole consequence, and it is printed here before the sale, not discovered after it.
  • The house owes a licensed call a working life: keeping the engines up and versioned is the house's obligation, and it is priced into the rate card rather than charged as a surprise.

E · What is NOT promised

  • No traffic. Driving customers to the store is the buyer's work.
  • No income. A stocked store is a starting position, not a result. Nobody at the house has told you this property will make any particular money, and this page outranks anybody who does.
  • No hidden second meter. Beyond the published rate card and the work you voluntarily call in, the house takes nothing from this property.
  • Checkout state, honestly: card checkout for individual applications opens with the house's licensed launch; today, application orders are taken by email and the house delivers the posted package by link — the same state the house's own depot carries. The buyer's own storefront checkout arrangements after transfer are the buyer's to wire.
